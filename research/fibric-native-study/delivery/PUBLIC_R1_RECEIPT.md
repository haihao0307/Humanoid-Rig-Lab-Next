## Geometry and limits

The executable generates 72 yarns, 2,304 surface filament curves, 3,200 tapered fuzz curves and 4,632,576 fabric triangles from the independent metric draft. Including the ground, the public scene has six meshes and 4,632,578 triangles. Default sample: 15.12 by 14.4 mm. No original teacher geometry, rendered frame or external model is loaded by the executable.

The 291,600 default sampled elliptical-envelope checks reported a maximum residual of 0.00008892318851706771 mm. This describes only the discrete bulk-envelope projection in flat material coordinates, NOT a continuous collision guarantee, fiber collision validation, or real cloth mechanics.

The actual desktop, macro and mobile screenshots were visually inspected. The sample remains too regular and ribbon-like relative to a cinematic textile reference, and the low-sample path-traced captures are visibly noisy. Neither three nor six samples proves convergence. The surface BSDF is not Karma Hair, the original Fibric pattern remains unavailable, and same-camera teacher fidelity has NOT passed. `cinematicParity=false`, `visualAcceptance=false`, `physicsSimulation=false`.

This is a functional public 3D research candidate, not completion of the user's top-tier cinematic target.
