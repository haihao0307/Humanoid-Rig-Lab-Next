# Source notices

The active character uses original analytic construction functions. No BodyParts3D mesh, SMPL asset, MediaPipe model, authored image texture, motion capture asset, or speech model is distributed in this snapshot.

Anatomy and physiology references are linked in `body/HumanBiology.json`, `body/SkinLayerProfiles.json`, `body/HairFollicles.js` and the source modules. They support relationships and statistical priors; character dimensions, schematic organs and simulation coefficients remain authored approximations. Full textbooks and research images are not included.

The project applies general ideas from the KAOPU / TLO / Object DNA charters in `haihao0307/guilin-dem-pipeline`; exact versions are recorded in `body/HumanDNAContract.json`. Their candidate status is preserved. No other Mother's production code is modified or bundled.

Optional speech dependencies are installed separately from the packages listed in `server/voice-requirements.txt` and retain their respective licenses. Their code, weights and user recordings are not included here. Historical implementations and their original notices remain in repository history.
