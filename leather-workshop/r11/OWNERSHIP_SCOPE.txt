KAOPU R11: authored work and license boundaries

The integer seed field and the R11 linking and test code were newly written
for Haihao's leather workbench. The algorithm was not obtained by renaming or
scalarizing the former hash helper. This is an engineering provenance record,
not a warranty of copyrightability, exclusive rights or global noninfringement.

The repository's existing MIT license remains in force for project code,
including the first publication of seed-field.glsl at commit
5ac2913da5565571aab50a64061894c38ff886fa. No prior grant is revoked. Self-authored
project code and exclusive commercial-use rights are different claims.

Third-party rendering, triangulation, recorded bending-derivative lineage,
and the CC0 scan textures retain their own rights and notices. Those components
are not described as original KAOPU source or exclusive scans. Full component
notices accompany this review build.

Any future proprietary-only module must be separated and have its licensing
scope decided BEFORE it is publicly released; copying MIT code into a new
folder or removing notices will not retract existing recipient rights.
Employee/contractor assignments, AI-related copyrightability, patent clearance,
and commercial deployment terms remain outside this technical verification.
